About

Creative director.
Storytelling designer.

We are the stories we tell. A never-ending stream of words with two goals: to fix our place in the world, and to meet those who can inhabit it with us.

My job is setting the floor plan and foundation, the color of the building, and most importantly, choosing how to speak to the roommates. Then figuring out what story we will tell, together.

My favorite word is “courage.” From the Provençal cour-age: to have a heart. It’s the point of many stories. Especially if we talk about companies. Umberto Morello
Manifesto

It is no accident
that they call them
enterprises.

We start with people, values, and experiences that add up over the years. Each enterprise is very close to a novel or TV series. One big plot, with branches, encounters, moments of crisis, and overwhelming victories.

To do this, we develop narrative lines, make sure they harmonize with every channel, and keep a steady course so the enterprise remains true to itself each time it is told.

The challenge? Telling yourself. A company that knows how to talk about itself wins before it even competes.
